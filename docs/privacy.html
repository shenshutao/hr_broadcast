HR Broadcast

Privacy Policy

Overview

HR Broadcast reads your heart rate from Apple Watch and broadcasts it locally over Bluetooth to nearby cycling computers (e.g. Garmin, Wahoo). We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external server.

Data We Access

  • Heart Rate — read from Apple Watch via a workout session. Used solely to broadcast over Bluetooth in real time. Never stored or sent off-device.
  • Bluetooth — used to advertise as a standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Profile sensor. No personal data is transmitted.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not have an account system or backend server.
  • Heart rate data never leaves your device except as anonymous Bluetooth broadcasts to devices you choose.

Third-Party Services

HR Broadcast displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

HR Broadcast is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
